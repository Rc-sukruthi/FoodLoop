Before you donate

Please make sure that:

  • The food is fresh and suitable for consumption.
  • It has been stored and handled safely.
  • The preparation or expiry information is accurate.
  • The quantity and pickup details are correct.
  • Food that is spoiled or unsafe is never donated.

Every donation counts!
